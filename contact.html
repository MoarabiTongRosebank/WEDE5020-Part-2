Visit Us

Whether you're stopping by for your morning coffee or picking up tomorrow's dinner bread, we'd love to see you at one of our two locations.

Copper Loaf Downtown

142 Mill Street, Riverside District

Phone: (021) 555-0142

Email: downtown@copperloafbakery.com

Opening Hours

  • Monday – Friday: 6:30 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Sunday: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Tucked beside the old grain mill, our downtown flagship is where it all began. Expect the low hum of the ovens, sun coming through the front windows, and a case full of whatever came out of the oven last.

Copper Loaf Northside

27 Orchard Lane, Northside Market

Phone: (021) 555-0198

Email: northside@copperloafbakery.com

Opening Hours

  • Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

Our newer, cozier location inside the Northside Market, perfect for a slower morning with coffee in hand and a pastry warm from the case.
