Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend at least three weeks' notice for standard celebration cakes, and six to eight weeks for wedding cakes or larger tiered designs. Popular dates in peak season book up quickly, so earlier is always better.

Do you offer cake tastings?

Yes. For wedding and large event cakes, we offer a paid tasting session where you can sample up to four flavor and filling combinations before making your final decision.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

We offer gluten-free and vegan cake options, and we can accommodate most common allergies with advance notice. Please note our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten, and eggs, so we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.

How much does a custom cake cost?

Pricing depends on size, design complexity, and flavor selections. Simple single-tier cakes typically start around R450, while elaborate tiered or wedding cakes are quoted individually after your enquiry.

Is a deposit required?

Yes, we require a 50% non-refundable deposit to confirm your booking, with the remaining balance due seven days before pickup or delivery.

Do you deliver cakes?

We offer local delivery and setup for an additional fee, calculated by distance. Pickup is also available at either of our two locations.

Can I make changes after submitting my enquiry?

Absolutely. Your enquiry is the start of the conversation, not the final word. We'll confirm every detail with you before your order is locked in.
